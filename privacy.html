Privacy policy / 隐私政策

Updated / 更新：2026-10-03

WristSip app

Your drink entries, labels, dates, daily goal, preferences and trial status are stored locally on your watch. WristSip does not upload raw drink records to a developer server. Core recording does not require a WristSip account or phone companion app.

The app includes no developer analytics or advertising SDK. Notification permission is used for reminders; declining it does not prevent recording. WristSip does not automatically detect drinking or provide medical advice.

Purchasing or restoring the one-time unlock contacts your platform's purchase service. Apple, Google or Huawei may process their own account and transaction information under their privacy policies. WristSip uses verified purchase results to determine unlock access; drink records are not sent as part of that process.

You can delete drink data in the app. Deleting drink entries does not revoke a store purchase. Removing or resetting the app/watch can erase local records; WristSip provides no cloud backup.
