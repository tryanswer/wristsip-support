WristSip support / 用户支持

Contact / 联系：support@fatclaw.com

Recording and local data

Open the watch app and choose a cup to record the amount you drank. Your daily goal is your own setting, not medical advice. History remains readable after the trial ends. You can correct or delete entries in history. Removing or resetting the app/watch can erase local records.

Trial and one-time unlock

The local trial lasts 21 × 24 hours from first use. It is not a subscription. After it ends, new records and reminders require the one-time unlock; history reading and drink-data deletion remain available. The purchase screen shows the store price. To restore an existing purchase, use “Restore purchase” on the unlock screen while connected to your store account. Store setup and availability depend on the platform; an unavailable purchase must not be treated as an unlock.

Reminders

Check notification permission, the active time window, pause settings and your daily goal. System quiet modes and background policies can affect delivery. Current Apple/Huawei schedules are finite; reopen the app to renew them. Reminders stop when the trial ends unless unlocked.

记录与本地数据

打开手表 App，选择杯量记录已经喝下的量。每日目标由你自行设置，不是医学建议。试用结束后历史仍可读，可在历史中更正或删除记录。卸载或重置 App、手表可能丢失本地记录。

试用与一次性买断

本地试用从首次使用起持续 21×24 小时，非订阅。到期后新增记录和提醒需买断，历史读取及删除饮水数据保留。付款时以商店显示的价格为准。恢复已有购买：联网并使用原商店账号，在买断页面选择“恢复购买”。商品配置及可用性取决于平台，购买不可用不代表已解锁。

提醒

检查通知权限、活跃时间窗、暂停设置和当日目标。系统静默模式及后台策略可能影响送达。当前 Apple/Huawei 预排队列有限，用尽需重新打开 App 续排；试用结束后未买断则停止提醒。

For help, include your watch model, OS version, WristSip version and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens or raw private health records.

反馈问题可提供手表型号、系统与 App 版本及复现步骤，不要发送密码、购买 token 或私密健康记录。
