Recording and local data

Open the watch app and choose a cup to record the amount you drank. Your daily goal is your own setting, not medical advice. All core functions are free with no trial deadline. You can correct or delete entries in history. Removing or resetting the app/watch can erase local records.

Free first release

Logging, history, cup presets, goals and reminders are free. No purchase, subscription or store connection is required for core use. Existing records and historical purchased rights are retained.

Reminders

Check notification permission, the active time window, pause settings and your daily goal. System quiet modes and background policies can affect delivery. Current Apple/Huawei schedules are finite; reopen the app to renew them.

记录与本地数据

打开手表 App，选择杯量记录已经喝下的量。每日目标由你自行设置，不是医学建议。完整核心功能免费且无试用期限，可在历史中更正或删除记录。卸载或重置 App、手表可能丢失本地记录。

免费首发版

记录、历史、杯量、目标和提醒均免费，无需购买、订阅或商店连接。现有记录和历史已购权益保留。

提醒

检查通知权限、活跃时间窗、暂停设置和当日目标。系统静默模式及后台策略可能影响送达。当前 Apple/Huawei 预排队列有限，用尽需重新打开 App 续排。

For help, include your watch model, OS version, WristSip version and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens or raw private health records.
